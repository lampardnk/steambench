## Truthful gaps retained

| Area | What remains unverified |
|---|---|
| Hungry for Mushrooms | The page's relic summary says Fragrant Mushroom upgrades 2 random cards, while its event option text says 3. The local note records both and defers to the live result. |
| The Merchant??? | The event page says 6 fake relics are randomly selected from a list but its rendered Relics section omits that list. Nine linked fake-relic pages were inspected and are recorded as candidates; the exact six-item selection list and the event page's unnamed Merchant-interaction exception list remain pending. |
| The Merchant??? / linked relics | The linked Venerable Tea Set??? page renders the Energy amount as an icon with no numeric value in extracted text; the local note preserves “additional Energy” without inventing a number. |
| Tinker Time | The page gives card types, base effects, and rider pools but does not state a generated Mad Science card cost. The local note leaves that field to the live preview. |
| Neow's newer relics | The Neow and Dowsing Rod pages carry a Beta Content Warning. The local note records the published effects and flags the installed-build check. |

No event-specific ascension substitutions were stated on the inspected event
pages. Parenthesized values in event-card descriptions are the source's
upgraded-card values; they are not silently treated as ascension modifiers.
