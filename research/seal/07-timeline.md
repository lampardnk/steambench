### This likely explains the "546" myth
The unverified Zhihu claim of "a20碎心546连胜" has a plausible origin: his
**2026-06-25** post reports an **SL** streak save sitting at **500-plus games with two
deaths** in STS2. That is a real, impressive, *save-load-permitted* figure — and it is
not an STS1 A20 NoSL streak. Still treat 546 as **[UNVERIFIED]**, but this is probably
the number that got garbled.
