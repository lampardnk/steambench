## He runs his own tournament too — 《每周日豹杯》 "Sunday Leopard Cup"

Easy to miss, and it reframes him as an organiser, not just a player. From his own
dynamics, at least **5 editions** ran from 2024-04 onward (weekly/biweekly Sundays, 6pm).

- **Format**: 8 players, single-elimination Bo1 (8→4→2→1, 7 games), rules inherited
  from 思豹尔杯; finals upgrade to Bo3 once someone is on a 2-title run.
- **Prize pool (his own money)**: ¥200 champion base + ¥100 per consecutive title,
  ¥40 participation × 7. Whoever *ends* a champion's streak immediately takes half the
  accumulated bonus, and the pot resets.
- **Handicap for winners**: a reigning champion loses one opening option for the whole
  next event, decided before each game — a nice anti-snowball rule.
- Related events he played in: **兔杯** (Rabbit Cup) — his 2025-02-03 championship VOD
  is an 87-minute NoSL triple-heart-kill. Its ruleset: A20, 1v1, NoSL speedrun, no
  re-roll of the opening 4, per-character time+seed budgets.

## Community footprint

- **《白夕Seal传奇》** — a fan-made "documentary" about him (101k views), widely
  referenced across the scene. Search that exact title on Bilibili.
- **233乐园 (233leyuan.com)** — a games aggregator that bulk-reposts Baidu Tieba /
  NGA threads about him. It is the most *searchable* source of streak commentary but
  **its post dates are scrape dates, not event dates** — this is exactly what makes
  the "38 and counting" figure look current when it isn't. Do not date events from it.
- **NGA 杀戮尖塔 board** — I got through the anti-bot guard and read the record thread
  (`tid=45494435`). It is an **STS1 A20-heart NoSL** snapshot from 2025-11, updated
  once (Watcher 57→64), and it says **nothing about STS2 or about him**. Useful as a
  historical baseline; it is *not* a live leaderboard, and there is no body that
  ratifies STS2 NoSL streaks.
- **Baidu Tieba 杀戮尖塔吧** — still captcha-gated; the aggregator reposts are the
  practical way in.
- A fan-made mod exists that replaces a character with him (`BV1yPXsB3EMD`).
