# Characters (general notes; check `sts2_wiki` for exact card text)

## Ironclad

- Starting relic heals 6 HP after every combat, so trading HP for value is acceptable early.
- Core plan: Strength scaling (Inflame, Demon Form, Spot Weakness), Block via Shrug It Off and Impervious,
  strong attacks (Bludgeon, Whirlwind with high energy, Heavy Blade with Strength).
- Exhaust synergy (Feel No Pain, Dark Embrace, Corruption) is powerful if the deck has Skills to exhaust.
- Early priorities: any good attack (Carnage, Uppercut, Pommel Strike, Twin Strike), one or two block cards,
  then a scaling Power by the end of Act 1.

## Silent (if available)

- Poison (Noxious Fumes, Catalyst), Shivs (Blade Dance, Accuracy), card draw and discard synergy.
- Very reliant on block cards (Footwork, Leg Sweep, Blur) because base HP is low.

## Defect (if available)

- Orbs: Lightning (damage), Frost (block), Dark (stored damage), Plasma (energy). Focus scales orbs.
- Defragment, Capacitor, Loop, Glacier and Cold Snap are core.

## Watcher (if available)

- Stances: Wrath doubles damage dealt and taken, Calm gives energy when exited, Divinity triples damage.
- Enter Wrath to kill, exit before enemies attack. Eruption/Vigilance loop; Talk to the Hand, Rushdown, Mental Fortress.

If the game offers a character not listed here, use `sts2_compendium` and `sts2_wiki` to learn its cards before
committing to a plan, and note what you learn in `scratchpad/`.
