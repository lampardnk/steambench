# Slay the Spire 2 basics

Slay the Spire 2 is a roguelike deck-builder. A run climbs the Spire act by act; each act is a map of nodes:
monsters (normal fights), elites (harder fights with a relic reward), rest sites (heal 30% max HP or upgrade a
card), shops, treasure, unknown events, and a boss at the top. Dying ends the run.

## Combat

- Each turn you draw 5 cards and get 3 energy (more with some relics). Cards cost energy to play.
- Attacks deal damage; Skills give Block or effects; Powers apply permanent effects for the fight.
- Block absorbs damage this turn only (it resets at the start of your turn unless a card or relic says otherwise).
- Enemy intents are shown: attack (with damage), defend, buff, debuff. Always compare incoming damage with
  current HP plus block. Kill or block before the enemy acts.
- Common status effects: Vulnerable (takes 50% more damage), Weak (deals 25% less), Frail (gains 25% less block),
  Strength (+damage per hit), Dexterity (+block per block card), Poison (loses HP per turn).
- Ending the turn discards your hand (cards with Retain stay). Exhausted cards are removed for the fight.

## Deck building

- After most fights you may add one of three cards or skip. Skipping is fine: a smaller deck draws its good cards
  more often. Take cards that fix a weakness: early damage to kill quickly, block for elites, a scaling plan for
  bosses (strength, powers, card draw, energy).
- Upgrading cards at rest sites is strong once your deck has a plan. Rest instead when below about 40% HP.
- Remove weak basic cards (Strike, Defend) at shops when you can afford it.
- Relics are permanent passive bonuses; potions are one-use and should be used in hard fights rather than hoarded.
- Ascension levels add difficulty (more enemy damage, fewer heals, harder elites). Ascension 1 adds more elites on the map.

## Priorities per act

- Act 1: build enough damage to beat elites (Gremlin Nob punishes Skills, Lagavulin needs burst damage,
  Sentries exhaust cards). Pick attack cards early.
- Act 2 and 3: develop scaling and defense; enemies hit much harder. Prefer synergy with your relics and powers.
- Always look at the map before choosing a path: plan rest sites before elites and the boss.
