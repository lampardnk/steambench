## Discard-for-slot guidance (editable)

Potion slots are scarce (2–3). Discard a potion when:
- Its best use has passed (e.g. Weak Potion after the multi-hit elite is dead).
- A better potion is offered and slots are full.
- The potion is character-inappropriate (Focus/Star/Orb potions for Ironclad).
- You're entering a rest site or shop and need a slot for a purchase.

**Discard candidates for Ironclad** (low-value): Focus Potion, Star Potion,
Poison Potion (low value vs Strength), Potion of Doom, Bone Brew, Heart of
Iron, King's Courage, Potion of Capacity, Essence of Darkness, Pot of Ghouls,
Cunning Potion.

## Ironclad potion priority in Act 1 (editable)

1. **Strength Potion** — your best stat; use in elite/boss.
2. **Energy Potion / Cure All / Radiant Tincture** — play more cards = win.
3. **Fire Potion** — 20 damage pushes lethal or kills a target.
4. **Vulnerable Potion** — combo with Bludgeon/Heavy Blade for +50% on a big hit.
5. **Block Potion / Fortifier / Ship in a Bottle** — block Dismember (26), Crash
   (16), Swoop (17), Slashes (13+).
6. **Weak Potion / Potion of Binding** — cut multi-hits (Peck 3×3, Lash 4×4,
   Thrash 3×3).
7. **Fairy in a Bottle** — carry into boss; safety net for lethal miscalculation.
8. **Entropic Brew** — refill slots; use when empty and a big fight is ahead.
