# Neow — Act 1 Ancient choice

Verified against slaythespire2.net beta (v0.111.0, display 2026-06-18) and
slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Neow. Neow appears in the first room
of Act 1, once the Neow epoch is revealed. Visiting an Ancient heals to full
(Ascension 2+ restores only 80% of missing HP).

## Choice structure

Each visit offers **3 relics**: one from the Curse (cost) pool, then two from
the Positive (no-cost) pool. The priced relic is always the third slot; the
other two never charge anything. The priced relic's weaker twin is removed
from the positive pool — you never see both a priced relic and its unpriced
twin in the same offer.

## Decision framework for Ironclad (editable)

### Scout the route first
Before choosing, look at the map (back button). Count elites, rest sites, shops.
- Many elites → Booming Conch (draw+energy in elite fights) is premium.
- Shop early → Golden Pearl (150 gold) funds removal.
- Few rest sites → Nutritious Oyster / Stone Humidifier (HP sustain).

### Assess the bundle, don't fix the transform
- New Leaf / Leafy Poultice transform a random card — you do NOT choose the
  target. Only take if every Strike/Defend is acceptable to lose (it is for
  Ironclad early). Never assume you'll transform your worst card.
- Precise Scissors / Precarious Shears remove a card YOU choose — reliable
  deck-thinning. Scissors (remove 1, free) is a safe Ironclad pick (remove a
  Strike). Shears (remove 2, -16 HP) is high-value but costs HP.
- Neow's Talisman (Upgrade 1 Strike + 1 Defend) is reliable value for Ironclad
  — upgraded Strike (9→12) and Defend (5→8) improve every combat.

### Ironclad tier picks (editable)
- **S**: Lost Coffer (card + potion), Phial Holster (potion slot + 2 potions),
  Booming Conch (elite fights), Neow's Talisman (upgrade Strike+Defend).
- **A**: Precise Scissors (remove Strike), Lava Rock (boss drops 2 relics),
  Golden Pearl (150 gold), Lead Paperweight (colorless flex).
- **B**: Neow's Torment (Neow's Fury is a good attack), Pomander (upgrade a
  card), Small Capsule (random relic).
- **C**: Arcane Scroll (random rare — variance high), New Leaf (transform —
  unreliable target), Nutritious Oyster (+11 Max HP — safe but low-impact).
- **Avoid unless you can pay**: Cursed Pearl (Greed is Eternal — bad for long
  fights), Leafy Poultice (-12 Max HP is a lot), Large Capsule (+2 basics
  dilute deck), Silken Tress (lose all gold).

### No fixed transform outcome
Transform (New Leaf, Leafy Poultice) produces a random card of any rarity. You
cannot predict or target the result. The transform preview animation re-rolls
~once per second but does NOT determine the result (see controls/CONTROLS.md:
"confirm immediately with y. It is not a timing challenge."). Choose transform
only when the worst-case outcome is acceptable.
