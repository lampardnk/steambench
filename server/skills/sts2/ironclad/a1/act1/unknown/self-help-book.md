# Self-Help Book

**Pool:** Any act  
**Appears when:** an option is shown only if you own a card it can enchant; [Move On] appears only when you own none

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Read the Back]** | Choose an Attack. Enchant it with **Sharp 2** (increases that card's damage by 2). |
| **[Read a Random Passage]** | Choose a Skill. Enchant it with **Nimble 2** (increases Block from that card by 2). |
| **[Read the Entire Book]** | Choose a Power. Enchant it with **Swift 2** (the first time you play it, draw 2 cards). |
| **[Move On]** | Nothing happens. |

## Mechanics

- Enchantments ride on one specific card. Transforming that card later discards the enchantment.

## What each option costs and buys

- Nothing here costs HP, gold or a card slot, so the only question is which of your existing cards compounds an enchantment best.
- Sharp and Nimble pay out every time the card is played; Swift pays once per combat and is worth most on a Power you play early.
- A card you may want to remove or transform later is a poor host for the enchantment.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
