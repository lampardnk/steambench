# Tea Master

**Pool:** Act 1, both biomes (also Act 2 Hive)  
**Appears when:** you have at least 150 Gold

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Bone Tea]** | Pay 50 Gold. Relic: at the start of the next combat, Upgrade your starting hand. |
| **[Ember Tea]** | Pay 150 Gold. Relic: at the start of the next 5 combats, gain 2 Strength. |
| **[Tea of Discourtesy]** | Free. Relic: at the start of the next combat, shuffle 2 Dazed into your Draw Pile. |

## Mechanics

- All three are Event Relics, so each counts toward relic totals even though two of them are consumed by the fights that follow.

## What each option costs and buys

- Ember Tea costs everything the event required you to bring and pays out only across the next 5 combats — it is worth most if elites or a boss fall inside that window, and least if the next five fights were easy anyway.
- Bone Tea is cheap and affects exactly one combat's opening hand; picking which fight it lands on is not in your control.
- Tea of Discourtesy is free and its listed effect is purely a downside — 2 Dazed in one combat — so the only thing it buys is the relic itself.
- Gold spent here is gold not spent on removal or relics at a shop later in the route.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
