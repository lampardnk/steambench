# Sunken Treasury

**Pool:** Underdocks  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[First Chest]** | Gain 52–68 Gold. |
| **[Second Chest]** | Gain 303–363 Gold. Add **Greed** (Unplayable, **Eternal** curse) to your deck. |

## Mechanics

- Greed is Eternal: it cannot be removed by any means for the rest of the run.

## What each option costs and buys

- The gap is roughly 250–310 Gold, which is shop-transforming money in Act 1.
- Greed is the harshest curse type here — Eternal means no shop removal, no removal event, no transform will ever clear it. You are carrying a dead card in every hand for the whole run.
- The trade gets worse the thinner your deck is and the longer the run continues, and better if you can convert the gold immediately into removals and relics that outpace one dead card.
