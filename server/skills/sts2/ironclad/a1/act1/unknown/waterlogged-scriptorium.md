# Waterlogged Scriptorium

**Biome:** Underdocks only. **Condition:** at least 55 Gold.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Bloody Ink]** | Gain 6 Max HP. |
| **[Tentacle Quill]** | Pay 55 Gold; enchant 1 card with **Steady**. Steady gives that card Retain. |
| **[Prickly Sponge]** | Pay 99 Gold; enchant 2 cards with **Steady**. |

Tentacle Quill and Prickly Sponge are locked when their Gold cost cannot be
paid. No event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare permanent Max HP
with the Gold costs and current enchantable cards.
