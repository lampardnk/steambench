# Waterlogged Scriptorium

**Pool:** Underdocks  
**Appears when:** you have at least 55 Gold

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Bloody Ink]** | Gain 6 Max HP. Free. |
| **[Tentacle Quill]** | Pay 55 Gold. Enchant a card with **Steady** (that card gains Retain). |
| **[Prickly Sponge]** | Pay 99 Gold. Enchant 2 cards with **Steady**. |

## Mechanics

- The two paid options are greyed out if you cannot afford them.
- Retain means the card stays in your hand between turns instead of being discarded.

## What each option costs and buys

- Bloody Ink is free and permanent, and needs none of the gold the event required you to bring.
- Retain is worth most on a card you want to hold for a specific turn — a big Block for a known incoming hit, or an expensive card you are saving energy for — and worth little on a card you play the moment you draw it.
- Prickly Sponge is 99 Gold for two enchantments, a better per-card rate than the Quill, but it requires two cards that genuinely want Retain.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
