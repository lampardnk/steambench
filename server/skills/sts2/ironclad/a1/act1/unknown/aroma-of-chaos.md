# Aroma of Chaos

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Let Go]** | Transform a card in your deck (you choose which card; the result is random). |
| **[Maintain Control]** | Upgrade a card in your deck (you choose which). |

## Mechanics

- A transform replaces a chosen card with a random card of any rarity. You pick the input, never the output.

## What each option costs and buys

- Both options are free — no HP, gold or card-count change — so this is purely transform variance against upgrade certainty.
- A transform is worth most when the input is a card you actively do not want, because any outcome is an improvement.
- An upgrade is worth most when you already own a card whose upgraded form is a large jump.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
