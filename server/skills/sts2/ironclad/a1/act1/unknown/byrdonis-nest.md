# Byrdonis Nest

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Eat the Egg]** | Gain 7 Max HP. |
| **[Take the Egg]** | Add **Byrdonis Egg** (Unplayable Quest card) to your deck. It can be hatched at a Rest Site. |

## Mechanics

- Hatching consumes a Rest Site action, which competes with healing or upgrading there.

## What each option costs and buys

- 7 Max HP is immediate, certain and never dilutes your deck.
- The egg is a dead card in every draw until you reach a Rest Site, and then costs that site's action to resolve.
- The egg's value depends on a Rest Site actually being on the route you intend to take.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
