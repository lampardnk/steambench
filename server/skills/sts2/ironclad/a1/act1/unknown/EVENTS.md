## Events shared by the Act 1 biomes

[The Sunken Statue](the-sunken-statue.md) can occur in both the Overgrowth and
the Underdocks. The following shared pages also apply in Act 1:

[Brain Leech](../../meta_strategy/unknown/brain-leech.md) ·
[Room Full of Cheese](../../meta_strategy/unknown/room-full-of-cheese.md) ·
[Self-Help Book](../../meta_strategy/unknown/self-help-book.md) ·
[Slippery Bridge](../../meta_strategy/unknown/slippery-bridge.md) ·
[Tea Master](../../meta_strategy/unknown/tea-master.md) ·
[The Future of Potions?](../../meta_strategy/unknown/the-future-of-potions.md) ·
[The Legends Were True](../../meta_strategy/unknown/the-legends-were-true.md) ·
[This or That?](../../meta_strategy/unknown/this-or-that.md)

These locations are taken from the individual wiki event pages. In particular,
Brain Leech is shared with Act 2 Hive but not Glory; Act 1 does not include the
Act 2/3-only events such as Crystal Sphere, nor the Glory-only War Historian,
Repy.

## Conditions listed by the wiki

| Gate | Event |
|---|---|
| At least 44 Gold | Whispering Hollow |
| At least 55 Gold | Waterlogged Scriptorium |
| At least 100 Gold and 2 transformable cards | Morphic Grove |
| At least 120 Gold | Endless Conveyor |
| At least 149 Gold | Luminous Choir |
| HP at or below 70% of Max HP | Unrest Site |
| Floor 6 or later | Punch Off |
| Floor 7 or later and a removable card | Slippery Bridge |
| At least 2 Potions | The Future of Potions? |
| At least 1 card and at least 10 HP | The Legends Were True |
| At least 1 card enchantable with Spiral | Spiraling Whirlpool |
| At least 1 basic card | Wood Carvings |

The pages for the remaining Act 1 events list no additional condition. Dense
Vegetation and Jungle Maze Adventure add a multiplayer gate of more than 8 HP
and more than 18 HP respectively for every player; those gates do not apply in
single-player. Conditions for the shared events live on their linked pages.
