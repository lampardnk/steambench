# Slippery Bridge

**Pool:** Any act  
**Appears when:** floor 7 or later, and you have at least 1 removable card

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Overcome]** | The named card is removed from your deck. |
| **[Hold On]** | Lose 3 HP and reroll which card is named. The cost rises by 1 each time (3, 4, 5, 6, 7...). Repeats until you Overcome. |

## Mechanics

- The named card is drawn at random from your cards without Eternal.
- The first card named is never Basic rarity unless every card you own is Basic. Basic cards can appear on rerolls.
- The same card is not named twice until every other card has been offered at least once.

## What each option costs and buys

- This is HP spent to aim a removal. The removal itself is free; only precision costs.
- Costs accumulate: two rerolls is 7 HP, three is 12, four is 18.
- Because the first name is never Basic, the opening offer is a real card rather than a Strike or Defend — which cuts both ways depending on whether you wanted a Basic removed.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
