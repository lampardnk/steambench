# Whispering Hollow

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** you have at least 44 Gold

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Exchange Gold]** | Lose 26–44 Gold. Procure 2 random Potions. |
| **[Hug the Tree]** | Lose 9 HP. Choose a card to Transform. |

## What each option costs and buys

- Two potions for under 44 Gold is cheaper than buying them, but only if you have free potion slots to hold them.
- The transform lets you pick the input, so it is worth most when you have a card you clearly want gone.
- 9 HP is the fixed price of the transform, and Ironclad's Burning Blood recovers 6 of it after the next combat.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
