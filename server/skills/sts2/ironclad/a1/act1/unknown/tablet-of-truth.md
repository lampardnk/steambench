# Tablet of Truth

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Smash]** | Heal 20 HP. |
| **[Decipher]** | Lose 3 Max HP. Upgrade a random card. Then an escalating ladder follows. |

## Mechanics

- Decipher stages: **2)** lose 6 Max HP, upgrade a random card. **3)** lose 12 Max HP, upgrade a random card. **4)** lose 24 Max HP, upgrade a random card. **5)** lose all but 1 Max HP, Upgrade **ALL** cards in your deck.
- **[Give Up]** is available at any stage and stops the ladder there.
- Each upgrade is a random card, not a chosen one — only the final stage is comprehensive.

## What each option costs and buys

- The ladder doubles each rung: 3, 6, 12, 24 Max HP for one random upgrade apiece, which is a steeply worsening rate.
- Cumulative cost through stage 4 is 45 Max HP for 4 random upgrades.
- Stage 5 upgrades the entire deck but leaves you at 1 Max HP, which makes every remaining hit lethal — it is only survivable with a plan that never takes damage.
- Smash is 20 current HP back with no permanent cost, and Max HP lost here is permanent while HP healed is not.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
