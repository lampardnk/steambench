# The Sunken Statue

**Pool:** Act 1, both biomes  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Grab the Sword]** | Obtain **Sword of Stone** (relic: transforms into a powerful Relic after defeating 5 Elites). |
| **[Dive into the Water]** | Gain 101–121 Gold. Lose 7 HP. |

## Mechanics

- The two options are exclusive — taking the sword destroys the statue and the pond loot with it.

## What each option costs and buys

- Sword of Stone is a bet on your whole route: 5 Elites is a commitment that spans acts, and the relic is inert until it pays.
- If you already intend to fight elites for relics, the sword costs nothing extra; if you plan to route around them, it never turns on.
- 101–121 Gold for 7 HP is the best gold-per-HP rate available in Act 1, and it is immediate and certain.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
