# Punch Off

**Pool:** Underdocks  
**Appears when:** floor 6 or later

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Nab]** | Add **Injury** (Unplayable curse) to your deck. Obtain a random Relic. |
| **[I Can Take Them]** | Fight **2 Punch Constructs**. On victory: a random Relic, a random Potion, and a standard combat reward. |

## Mechanics

- Each Punch Construct starts damaged, missing between 2 and 9 HP.
- See `act1/normal/UNDERDOCKS.md` for the Punch Construct rotation — it opens with 10 Block, applies Frail, then hits for 14.

## What each option costs and buys

- Both options give a random relic. The fight adds a potion and a full combat reward on top; Nab adds a permanent curse instead.
- The fight is two enemies that each block on their opening turn and apply Frail before their big hit, which is a real HP cost even when won.
- Injury is Unplayable and not self-removing, so it dilutes every draw for the rest of the run unless you spend a removal on it.
- Your current HP and whether a Rest Site follows are what separate these, not the rewards.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
