# Abyssal Baths

**Pool:** Underdocks  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Immerse]** | Gain 2 Max HP. Take 3 damage. A ladder then follows. |
| **[Abstain]** | Heal 10 HP. |

## Mechanics

- After Immerse, **[Linger]** repeats: gain 2 Max HP and take escalating damage — 4, then 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and upward, +1 each time.
- **[Exit Baths]** is available at every stage and stops the ladder there.
- The game warns you on the last rung before the damage would kill you.

## What each option costs and buys

- Each rung trades current HP for permanent Max HP at a worsening rate: the first is 2 Max HP for 3 damage, the fourth is 2 for 6.
- Cumulative: four rungs is 8 Max HP for 18 damage. The Max HP is permanent; the damage is not, and Ironclad's Burning Blood returns 6 per combat.
- The ceiling is your current HP, so the ladder is longest when you are healthy and unavailable when you are not.
- Abstain is the opposite trade — current HP back, no permanent gain.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
