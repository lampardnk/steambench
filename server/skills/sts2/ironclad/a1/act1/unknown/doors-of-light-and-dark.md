# Doors of Light and Dark

**Pool:** Underdocks  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Light Door]** | Upgrade 2 random cards. |
| **[Dark Door]** | Remove 1 card from your deck. |

## Mechanics

- The two upgrades are random; the removal is chosen by you.

## What each option costs and buys

- Both are free — no HP, no gold — so this is two random upgrades against one chosen removal.
- Upgrades scale with how many of your cards are worth upgrading; in a deck still full of Strikes and Defends, two random upgrades often land on cards you would rather delete.
- A removal is worth more in a deck you are trying to thin, and the pick is yours rather than the game's.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
