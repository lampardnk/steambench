# Wood Carvings

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** you have at least 1 basic card

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Bird]** | Choose 1 starter card to Transform into **Peck** (1 Energy, Colorless Attack: deal 2 damage 3 times; 4 times upgraded). |
| **[Snake]** | Enchant 1 card with **Slither** (when you draw this card, randomize its cost from 0 to 3). Locked if no card can take it. |
| **[Torus]** | Choose 1 starter card to Transform into **Toric Toughness** (2 Energy, Colorless Skill: gain 5 Block, and 5 Block at the start of the next 2 turns; 7 upgraded). |

## Mechanics

- Transforming a card discards any enchantment it was carrying.
- Unlike most transforms, these two name the exact output — Bird and Torus are deterministic.

## What each option costs and buys

- Bird and Torus are rare transforms with a known result, which removes the usual variance argument against transforming.
- Peck deals 6 damage across **3 separate instances**, which is small raw damage but useful against anything that caps or counts damage instances rather than totals.
- Toric Toughness is 15 Block spread over three turns for one card and 2 Energy, which suits fights with a known big hit a turn or two away.
- Slither is a gamble in both directions: the card may cost 0 or may cost 3, rerolled on every draw.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
