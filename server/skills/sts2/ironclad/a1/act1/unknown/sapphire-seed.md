# Sapphire Seed

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Consume]** | Heal 9 HP. Upgrade a card in your deck. |
| **[Plant and Nourish]** | Enchant a card with **Sown** (the first time you play this card each combat, gain Energy). |

## Mechanics

- Sown rides on one card; transforming that card later loses the enchantment.

## What each option costs and buys

- Consume is two certain benefits at once — a heal and an upgrade — with no downside.
- Sown is an energy engine that repeats every combat for the rest of the run, which scales with how many combats remain and how reliably you draw the host card.
- Sown is worth most on a cheap card you play early every fight, and least on a situational one you often do not play.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
