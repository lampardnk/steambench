# Wellspring

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Bottle]** | Procure 1 random Potion. |
| **[Bathe]** | Remove 1 card from your deck. Add 1 **Guilty** (Unplayable curse) to your deck. |

## Mechanics

- Guilty removes itself from your deck after 5 combats, so its cost is bounded rather than permanent.

## What each option costs and buys

- Bathe is a free removal rented against a curse that expires on its own — net deck size is unchanged, and after 5 combats you are simply one card lighter.
- The 5 combats during which Guilty clogs your draws are the ones happening now, which may include an elite or the Act 1 boss.
- Bottle is small, immediate and carries no deck cost at all.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
