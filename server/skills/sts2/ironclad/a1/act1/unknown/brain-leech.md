# Brain Leech

**Pool:** Act 1, both biomes (also Act 2 Hive)  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Share Knowledge]** | Choose 1 of 5 random cards to add to your deck. |
| **[Rip the Leech Off]** | Lose 5 HP. Gain a **Colorless** card reward. |

## What each option costs and buys

- Both options add exactly one card, so this is a question of which pool you would rather pick from, not whether to grow the deck.
- Five random cards is a wider pick than a Colorless reward, and costs no HP.
- Colorless cards are off-class utility you cannot otherwise draft; the 5 HP is the price of reaching that pool.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
