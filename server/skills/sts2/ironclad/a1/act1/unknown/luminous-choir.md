# Luminous Choir

**Pool:** Overgrowth  
**Appears when:** you have at least 149 Gold

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Reach into the Flesh]** | Remove **2 cards** from your deck. Add **Spore Mind** (1 Energy curse, Exhaust) to your deck. |
| **[Offer Tribute]** | Pay 100–149 Gold. Obtain a random Relic. |

## Mechanics

- Spore Mind costs 1 Energy and Exhausts, so it can be cleared from a hand once for an energy rather than sitting there all combat.

## What each option costs and buys

- Two removals in one event is the largest thinning available in Act 1, and the curse attached is among the mildest kinds — playable away and self-exhausting.
- Net deck change is −1 card, and the two you remove are chosen by you while the curse is not.
- Offer Tribute converts a large amount of gold into an unknown relic; the price band is wide, and at the low end it is much better value than at the high end.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
