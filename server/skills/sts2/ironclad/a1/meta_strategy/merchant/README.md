## Purchase Priority (Strategic Inference)

{strategic inference} General considerations for shop purchases:
- Card removal is the most consistent power increase but gets expensive after repeated uses (base 75, +25 each; A6+: base 100, +50 each).
- Relics provide permanent passive effects and are the strongest permanent investment.
- Key Ironclad cards (Offering, Dark Embrace, Inflame, Impervious) may be worth buying if missing from the deck.
- Colorless cards (Apotheosis, Hidden Gem, Enlightenment) are available to any character and may fill build gaps.
- Potions are consumables — buy only when preparing for a specific hard fight and you have an empty slot.

No universal "always buy" or "never buy" list applies across all runs. Evaluate based on gold available, upcoming fights, and current deck composition.

## Ascension Impact on Merchant (Sourced)

Source: slaythespire2.net Ascension table

At A6+ (Inflation):
- Base removal cost: 100 gold (up from 75).
- Per-removal increase: 50 gold (up from 25).
- Combined with reduced gold income from A3 (Poverty), repeated removal becomes significantly more expensive.
