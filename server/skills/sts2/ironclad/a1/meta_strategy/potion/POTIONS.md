# Potions — Act 1 strategy

Effects verified against slaythespire2.net beta (v0.111.0, display 2026-06-18);
the rules below are from slaythespire.wiki.gg. 62 potions: 20 Common, 20
Uncommon, 20 Rare, plus 2 Event potions.

## The rule that matters most

**Using a potion costs no Energy and does not count as playing a card.**

That single line decides more fights than any individual potion, because every
card-play restriction in Act 1 leaves potions untouched:

- **Ringing** (Ceremonial Beast, "you can only play 1 card this turn") — you may
  still use potions on top of that one card.
- **Smoggy** (Living Fog, "1 Skill per turn") — potions are not Skills.
- Energy denial and cost inflation (Tangled, Borrowed Time) do not touch them.

So the turns where your hand is most restricted are exactly the turns a potion
is worth the most. A potion held back "for the boss" and never spent is worth
nothing at all.

## Slots, odds and where potions come from

- **3 slots** by default, **2 at A4+**. Only Potion Belt, Alchemical Coffer (+4,
  each filled) and Phial Holster (+1, plus 2 potions) raise that.
- **Rarity when one drops:** Common 65% · Uncommon 25% · Rare 10%.
- **Drop chance after combat starts at 40%** and self-corrects: it falls 10%
  when a potion drops and rises 10% when one does not, so a dry run makes the
  next drop likelier. Elites carry a **+12.5%** bonus. White Beast Statue
  guarantees a drop without eating into those odds.
- **Usable outside combat — only these:** Ambergris, Entropic Brew, Fruit Juice,
  Foul Potion (only thrown at the Merchant), and **Blood Potion (Ironclad
  only)**. Everything else is combat-only, so it cannot rescue you on the map.
- Ironclad's three exclusive potions, one per rarity: **Blood Potion**,
  **Ashwater**, **Soldier's Stew**.
- Alchemize and Entropic Brew cannot create Fairy in a Bottle, Fruit Juice or
  Regen Potion **during** combat; Entropic Brew can outside it.

Potions are one-use; discard to free a slot for a better one.

## Event potions (2)
- Foul Potion: 12 dmg to ALL (players + enemies); throw at Merchant for 100 gold.
- Glowwater Potion: Exhaust hand, draw 10. (High-risk, high-reward cycle.)

## What a slot costs

Potion slots are 2-3 for most of a run, so holding a potion has a price: the
next one offered is refused, or something is dropped to take it. The facts that
bear on which:

- A potion whose window has passed still occupies its slot - Weak Potion after
  the multi-hit fight it was held for, for instance.
- Focus, Star and Orb potions do nothing for Ironclad, which has no Orbs.
  Poison Potion, Potion of Doom, Bone Brew, Heart of Iron, King's Courage,
  Potion of Capacity, Essence of Darkness, Pot of Ghouls and Cunning Potion are
  the others whose text does least with an Ironclad deck - read what each one
  actually says against the deck you have rather than treating that as a
  discard list.
- A shop or a rest site can offer potions, so arriving with a full belt means
  refusing whatever is there.

## Matching a potion to a problem

Not a ranking — the same potion is decisive in one fight and dead in the next.
Match what you are holding to the problem the fight actually poses.

| The problem | What answers it |
|---|---|
| One telegraphed big hit incoming | Block Potion (12), Fortifier (triples Block), Ship in a Bottle (10 now + 10 next), Ghost in a Jar (Intangible: everything becomes 1) |
| A damage race you are losing | Fire Potion (20), Explosive Ampoule (10 to ALL), Strength Potion, Flex Potion, Gigantification with your largest single hit |
| Enemy Strength snowballing | Shackling Potion (ALL enemies −7 this turn), Weak Potion, Beetle Juice (−30% for 4 turns) |
| A hand you cannot pay for | Energy Potion, Cure All, Radiant Tincture — and remember none of these cost a card play |
| A restricted turn (Ringing, Smoggy) | Anything at all — the restriction does not apply to potions |
| Deck clogged with Statuses | Ashwater, Glowwater Potion |
| A swarm | Explosive Ampoule, Potion of Binding |
| Miscalculating lethal | Fairy in a Bottle, Lucky Tonic |
| A damage cap or counter (Slippery, Hardened Shell) | avoid single-burst potions here — Fire Potion is one instance capped like any other |

Character-locked potions are the only reliably safe discards for Ironclad: Focus
Potion, Star Potion, Potion of Capacity, Essence of Darkness, King's Courage,
Bone Brew, Pot of Ghouls. Everything else has some fight where it is the answer.
