# The Legends Were True

**Pool:** Act 1, both biomes  
**Appears when:** you have at least 1 card in your deck and at least 10 HP

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Nab the Map]** | Receive **Spoils Map** (Unplayable Quest card: marks a site of 600 extra Gold in the next Act). |
| **[Slowly Find an Exit]** | Lose 8 HP. Procure 1 random Potion. |

## Mechanics

- Spoils Map sits in your deck as an unplayable card until the next Act, where it is consumed.

## What each option costs and buys

- 600 Gold is far more than any other Act 1 gold source, but it arrives in Act 2 and costs you a dead card in every draw until then.
- The dead card is worst in a thin deck, where it displaces a larger share of your draws, and in fights you must win now.
- 8 HP for one random potion is immediate and small in both directions.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
