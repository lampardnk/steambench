# Doll Room

**Biome:** Hive only.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Pick at Random]** | Obtain 1 random Doll Relic. |
| **[Take Some Time]** | Lose 5 HP; choose 1 of 2 Doll Relics. |
| **[Examine Each and Make the Best Choice]** | Lose 15 HP; choose 1 of 3 Doll Relics. |

The Doll Relic pool is:

- **Bing Bong:** whenever you add a card to your Deck, add one additional copy.
- **Daughter of the Wind:** whenever you play an Attack, gain 1 Block.
- **Mr. Struggles:** at the start of your turn, deal damage equal to the turn number to all enemies.

No additional condition or event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare
the current-HP cost with the number of choices and the relic effects.
