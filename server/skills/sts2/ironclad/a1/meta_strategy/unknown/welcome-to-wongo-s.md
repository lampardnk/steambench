# Welcome to Wongo's

**Biome:** Hive only. **Condition:** at least 100 Gold.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Wongo's Bargain Bin]** | Pay 100 Gold; obtain 1 random Common Relic and receive 32 Wongo Points. |
| **[Wongo's Featured Item]** | Pay 200 Gold; obtain a named Rare Relic randomly selected from the standard Rare Relic pool and receive 16 Wongo Points. |
| **[Wongo's Mystery Box]** | Pay 300 Gold; obtain **Wongo's Mystery Ticket** and receive 8 Wongo Points. The ticket gives 3 random Relics after 5 combats. |
| **[Leave]** | Downgrade a random card. |

## Notes [wiki-driven]

The Featured Item cannot be blacklisted from the Merchant and is displayed by
name. Wongo Points persist across the profile's runs and characters. At 2000
total points, the player receives **Wongo Customer Appreciation Badge**, which
does nothing. No separate event ascension variant is listed.

Compare the fixed Gold costs and random relic outcomes with current state; use
the live displayed featured relic and point total.
