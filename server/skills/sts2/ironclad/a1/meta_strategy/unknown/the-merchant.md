# The Merchant???

**Biomes:** Hive and Glory. **Acts:** 2 and 3 only. **Not available in
multiplayer.** **Condition:** at least 100 Gold **or** a held Foul Potion.

## Fake shop and combat

The shop contains 6 knock-off Relics, each priced at a random 43–57 Gold. The
wiki page says the 6 are randomly chosen from the list below, but its extracted
Relics section omits the list; the linked relic pages expose these 9 candidates:

| Knock-off Relic | Exact effect |
|---|---|
| Anchor??? | Start each combat with 4 Block. |
| Blood Vial??? | At the start of each combat, heal 1 HP. |
| Happy Flower??? | Every 5 turns, gain 1 Energy; its counter persists between combats. |
| Lee's Waffle??? | On pickup, heal 10% of Max HP. The linked page's short description says “10% of your HP”; verify the displayed wording in-game. |
| Mango??? | On pickup, raise Max HP by 3. |
| Orichalcum??? | If you end your turn without Block, gain 3 Block. It stacks with Orichalcum for 9 total Block. |
| Snecko Eye??? | Start each combat Confused; cards have their costs randomized from 0 to 3 on draw. |
| Strike Dummy??? | Cards containing “Strike” deal 1 additional damage. |
| Venerable Tea Set??? | Whenever you enter a Rest Site, start the next combat with an additional Energy. |

If a held **Foul Potion** is thrown at the fake Merchant, combat starts. Victory
gives 300 Gold, lets you take the relics that were for sale, and gives
**The Merchant's Rug???**, which does nothing; each displayed relic can be
skipped.
