# Stone of All Time

**Biome:** Hive only. **Act:** 2. **Condition:** at least 1 Potion.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Drink and Lift]** | Lose a random Potion; gain 10 Max HP. |
| **[Push]** | Lose 6 HP; enchant an Attack with **Vigorous 8**. Vigorous makes the first play of that card deal 8 additional damage. |

## Notes [wiki-driven]

It is not possible to consume or discard a Potion during this event. No separate
event ascension variant is listed. Compare the permanent Max HP gain or attack
enchantment with the current Potion and HP state.
