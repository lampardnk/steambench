# Room Full of Cheese

**Biomes:** Overgrowth, Underdocks, and Hive.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Gorge]** | Choose 2 of 8 random Common cards to add to your Deck. The 8 cards contain no duplicates. |
| **[Search]** | Lose 14 HP; obtain **The Chosen Cheese**. At end of combat, it gives 1 Max HP. |

## Notes [wiki-driven]

- Remaining Silver Crucible charges are not consumed by **[Gorge]**, and the cards are not upgraded.
- With Prismatic Gem, **[Gorge]** generates only Commons from the base class.
- Each character has 20 Common cards, so the wiki gives a 40% chance for any specific Common to appear.

No separate event ascension variant is listed. Compare the current-HP relic
cost with the card reward and current deck.
