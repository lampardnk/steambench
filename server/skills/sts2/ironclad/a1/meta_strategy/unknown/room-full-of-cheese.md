# Room Full of Cheese

**Pool:** Act 1, both biomes (also Act 2 Hive)  
**Appears when:** no condition

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Gorge]** | Choose 2 of 8 random **Common** cards to add to your deck. |
| **[Search]** | Lose 14 HP. Obtain **The Chosen Cheese** (relic: at the end of combat, gain 1 Max HP). |

## Mechanics

- The 8 offered cards contain no duplicates.
- Gorge does not consume Silver Crucible charges and the offered cards are not upgraded by it.
- Each character has 20 Commons, so any specific Common has a 40% chance to appear among the 8.

## What each option costs and buys

- Gorge adds two cards for free — deck thickness against two picks from a 40%-coverage slice of your Commons.
- 14 HP is among the steepest single costs in Act 1, and The Chosen Cheese only repays it across many later combats: it needs 14 more fights just to break even on Max HP, though the Max HP it grants is permanent and compounding.
- The Cheese is worth more the earlier you take it and the longer the run is expected to last.

Neither option is the correct one in the abstract. Weigh the exact costs above against this run's HP, max HP, gold, deck size and remaining route, and decide here.
