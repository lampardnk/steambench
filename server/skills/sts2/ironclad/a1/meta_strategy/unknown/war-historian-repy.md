# War Historian, Repy

**Biome:** Glory only. This is the follow-up to **The Lantern Key** in the
Hive. **Condition:** at least 1 Lantern Key in your Deck and this event has not
already been encountered. When eligible, every Glory room is this event;
otherwise it never appears.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Unlock the Cage]** | Lose 1 **Lantern Key**; obtain **History Course**. History Course plays a copy of your last-played Attack at the start of your turn. |
| **[Unlock the Chest]** | Lose 1 **Lantern Key**; procure 2 random Potions and obtain 2 random Relics. |

## Notes [wiki-driven]

The first choice consumes one Lantern Key. In single-player, if a duplicated
Lantern Key remains, the other option must also be chosen; all remaining copies
are then removed. This duplicate interaction is unavailable in multiplayer.
Lantern Key is an unplayable Quest card that unlocks a special event in the next
Act. No separate event ascension variant is listed.

Compare the relic with the two-potion/two-relic bundle and current Lantern Key
count.
