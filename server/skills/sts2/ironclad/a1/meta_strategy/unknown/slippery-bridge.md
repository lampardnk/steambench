# Slippery Bridge

**Acts:** Any Act. **Condition:** floor 7 or later and at least 1 removable
card.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Overcome]** | Remove the specific card named by the event. |
| **[Hold On]** | Lose 3 HP and randomize the named card. The event repeats until **[Overcome]**; each Hold On increases its HP cost by 1 (3, 4, 5, 6, ...). |

## Mechanics [wiki-driven]

The card is chosen from all cards without Eternal. The first offered card is
never Basic unless every card is Basic; Basic cards can appear on rerolls. The
same card is not offered twice unless the event has already offered every other
card in the Deck at least once.

No separate event ascension variant is listed. Compare the escalating current-HP
cost with the removal target and follow the live option text.
