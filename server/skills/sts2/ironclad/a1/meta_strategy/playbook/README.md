# Decision worksheet

This file supplies questions, not a card tier list or a prescribed route. Live
state and the exact source-linked facts in the other notes decide the answer.

## Before acting

1. Identify the current screen and the irreversible choice it exposes.
2. Record the relevant live resources: HP, Max HP, Block, Energy, gold, cards,
   potions, relic counters, map access, intents and status amounts.
3. Read the exact card, relic, Potion, event or service text.
4. Calculate the immediate effect, its cost, timing and reachable follow-up.
5. State the uncertainty and choose the option whose risk/reward fits this run.
6. Re-read after the action; a changed hand, target, pile, modifier, intent or
   screen invalidates a plan based on the previous observation.

## Combat questions

Use the [Keywords](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Keywords),
[Buffs](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs) and
[Debuffs](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Debuffs) pages to
resolve timing and stack behaviour. Compare damage, Block, HP loss, Energy,
draw, pile movement, enemy intent and turns-to-lethal from current numbers.
Do not plan around an unobserved draw or random outcome.

## Map, shop and rest questions

Use [Map Locations](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Map_Locations),
[The Merchant](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:The_Merchant)
and [Rest Sites](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Rest_Sites)
for the location's factual rules. Compare the current route, price or service
with HP, gold, deck composition, potions, relic effects and future reachable
options. These notes do not set an HP threshold or a purchase/upgrade priority.

## Learning

Durable learning describes a reusable mechanic, trigger, timing, enemy intent
graph or source uncertainty. Mark a community wiki Notes/Interactions observation
`[wiki-driven]` and link the exact page. Keep a run's offers, route, floor,
rounds, hand, HP, gold and outcome in the scratchpad only.
