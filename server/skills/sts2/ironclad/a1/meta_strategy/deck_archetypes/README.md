# Deck composition

“Archetype” is a shorthand for cards that share an effect or condition. It is
not a guarantee that a run should pursue that group. The [Ironclad page](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Ironclad)
and [Cards List](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Cards_List)
are the catalog; the live offer and deck are authoritative.

## Effect groupings

| Grouping | Text features to identify | Questions for the current run |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Cards or relics that gain Strength, or attacks whose damage is computed per hit. | How much Strength is present, when it arrives, and how many legal hits can use it? |
| Exhaust | Cards that Exhaust themselves or other cards, or effects that trigger when a card is Exhausted. | Which cards leave the combat deck, which payoffs trigger, and what remains available later in the fight? |
| Block | Cards that gain Block, preserve it, or convert Block into another effect. | What damage do the visible intents require, and how does the live Block modifier affect each instance? |
| Self HP loss | Cards or relics whose text loses HP or responds to HP loss. | What is the exact HP loss, what observed effect follows, and can the current route absorb it? |
| Vulnerable or Weak | Cards that apply or respond to these debuffs. | What duration and target are shown, and which later cards actually use the debuff? |
| Multi-enemy or multi-hit | Effects that target ALL enemies or create multiple hit instances. | What are the live targets, per-hit modifiers and enemy powers? |
| Draw or Energy | Effects that draw cards, add Energy or change a cost. | Which reachable cards can be paid for after this effect, and what uncertainty does the draw introduce? |

The same card can belong to several groups. A label does not establish a build,
an order of play or a value ranking.

## Reading a proposed combination

Use the current deck and visible encounter to calculate the effect chain. Check
the [Keywords](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Keywords),
[Buffs](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs),
[Debuffs](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Debuffs) and
[Mechanics](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Mechanics) pages
when timing, stack type, pile movement or card modifiers matter. Re-read live
text after a modifier, upgrade, transform or generated card appears.

## Reusable learning

A durable note may describe a card interaction with its condition and exact
source. It must say where the interaction stops or remains uncertain. Do not
call one grouping “winning”, record a fixed draft priority, or copy one run's
deck, offers, map or outcome into this file.
