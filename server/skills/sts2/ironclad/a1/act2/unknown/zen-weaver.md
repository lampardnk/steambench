# Zen Weaver

**Biome:** Hive only. **Condition:** at least 125 Gold.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Breathing Techniques]** | Pay 50 Gold; add 2 **Enlightenment** cards. Each is a 0-energy Event Skill that reduces the cost of all cards in your Hand to 1 this turn in combat, then Exhausts. |
| **[Emotional Awareness]** | Pay 125 Gold; remove 1 card from your Deck. |
| **[Arachnid Acupuncture]** | Pay 250 Gold; remove 2 cards from your Deck. |

Emotional Awareness and Arachnid Acupuncture are locked when their Gold costs
cannot be paid. No event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare the
costs and deck changes with current Gold and deck state.
