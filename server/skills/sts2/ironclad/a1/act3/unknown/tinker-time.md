# Tinker Time

**Biome:** Glory only.

This event creates one custom **Mad Science** card. Two of the three card types
are offered at random, then two rider effects from the chosen type's pool are
offered at random.

## Card type step

| Option | Card created |
|---|---|
| **[Weapon]** | An Attack that deals 12 damage. |
| **[Protector]** | A Skill that gains 8 Block. |
| **[Gadget]** | A Power. |

## Rider step

| Type | Possible riders and exact effects |
|---|---|
| Attack | **Sapping:** apply 2 Weak and 2 Vulnerable. **Violence:** hit 2 additional times. **Choking:** whenever you play a card this turn, the enemy loses 6 HP. |
| Skill | **Energized:** gain 2 Energy. **Wisdom:** draw 3 cards. **Chaos:** add a random card to your Hand; it is free this turn. |
| Power | **Expertise:** gain 2 Strength and 2 Dexterity. **Curious:** Powers cost 1 less. **Improvement:** at end of combat, upgrade a random card. |
