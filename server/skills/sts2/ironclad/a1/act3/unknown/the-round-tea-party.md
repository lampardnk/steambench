# The Round Tea Party

**Biome:** Glory only. **Condition:** at least 12 HP.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Enjoy Your Tea]** | Obtain **Royal Poison** and heal to full HP. Royal Poison makes you lose 4 HP at the start of each combat. |
| **[Pick a Fight]** | Lose 11 HP; obtain a random Relic. |

No event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare the full heal plus
ongoing relic cost with the immediate HP loss and random relic.
