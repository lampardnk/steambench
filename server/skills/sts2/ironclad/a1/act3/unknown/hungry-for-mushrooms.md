# Hungry for Mushrooms

**Biome:** Glory only.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Big Mushroom]** | Obtain **Big Mushroom**. On pickup, raise Max HP by 20; at the start of each combat, draw 2 fewer cards. |
| **[Fragrant Mushroom]** | Obtain **Fragrant Mushroom**. On pickup, lose 15 HP and upgrade random cards. |
