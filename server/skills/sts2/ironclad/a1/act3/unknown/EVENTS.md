## Conditions listed by the wiki

| Gate | Event |
|---|---|
| At least 100 Gold | Crystal Sphere, The Merchant??? |
| At least 1 Potion | Ranwid the Elder (also needs Gold and a tradable Relic) |
| At least 5 tradable Relics | Relic Trader |
| At least 12 HP | The Round Tea Party |
| At least 1 card enchantable with Soul's Power | Grave of the Forgotten |
| At least 2 Potions | The Future of Potions? |
| At least 1 removable card | Slippery Bridge |

The Merchant??? can also appear with a Foul Potion even without 100 Gold; it
cannot appear in Multiplayer. War Historian, Repy appears only when its Lantern
Key condition is met. Read each linked note for the complete condition and
any missing gate.
