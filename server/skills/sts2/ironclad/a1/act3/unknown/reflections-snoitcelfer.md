# Reflections snoitcelfeR

**Biome:** Glory only. **Unlock:** the **They Return Epoch**.

## Options

| Option | Exact outcome |
|---|---|
| **[Touch a Mirror]** | Downgrade 2 random cards; upgrade 4 random cards. |
| **[Shatter]** | Duplicate your entire Deck; add **Bad Luck** (curse). Bad Luck is unplayable, Eternal, and at end of turn loses 13 HP when in your Hand. |

## Notes [wiki-driven]

- The wiki notes that a card downgraded by **[Touch a Mirror]** can immediately be upgraded again.
- With relics such as Frozen Egg or Fresnel Lens, duplicated cards can become upgraded or enchanted even when their originals were not.
- The page also records Book of Five Rings as healing a large amount, Lucky Fysh as gaining a large Gold amount, and Bing Bong as causing an extra deck duplication and a second Bad Luck. Verify these interactions against the live build.

No event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare the deck-size change and
curse with current state.
