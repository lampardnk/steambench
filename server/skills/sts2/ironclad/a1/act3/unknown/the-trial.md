# The Trial

**Biome:** Glory only.

## Accept and verdicts

After **[Accept]**, one of the three trials is selected at random.

| Trial | Verdict | Exact outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant | **[DECIDE: Guilty]** | Add **Regret**; obtain 2 random Relics. |
| Merchant | **[DECIDE: Innocent]** | Add **Shame**; upgrade 2 cards. |
| Noble | **[DECIDE: Guilty]** | Heal 10 HP. |
| Noble | **[DECIDE: Innocent]** | Add **Regret**; gain 300 Gold. |
| Nondescript | **[DECIDE: Guilty]** | Add **Doubt**; gain 2 card rewards. |
| Nondescript | **[DECIDE: Innocent]** | Add **Doubt**; Transform 2 cards. |

The curse effects listed by the wiki are: **Regret** is unplayable and loses 1
HP at end of turn for each card in Hand; **Shame** is unplayable and gives 1
Frail at end of turn while in Hand; **Doubt** is unplayable and gives 1 Weak
at end of turn while in Hand.

## Reject path

**[Reject]** is unavailable. The event asks again with **[Double Down]** in its
place; selecting it instantly kills the player and it is not selectable in
multiplayer.

No additional condition or event-specific ascension variant is listed. Compare
the verdict's fixed outcome and curse with current state.
